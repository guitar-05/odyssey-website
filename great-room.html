A place to think

Where serious study feels like a privilege

Odyssey students study at The Great Room, a refined workspace in Gaysorn Tower at the heart of Bangkok's Ratchaprasong district. Quiet, light-filled and professional, it treats every student as the young scholar they are becoming.

A day at Odyssey

Four hours of focus.
The rest is theirs.

A flexible, hybrid study plan built around concentration rather than hours on a timetable.

  1. Morning

    Deep study

    University-level coursework in a calm, focused setting.

  2. Midday

    Mentor check-in

    Progress, questions and next steps with a dedicated mentor.

  3. Afternoon

    A life beyond study

    Sport, art, research projects, family. The time students need to grow.

  4. Anywhere

    Remote access

    When the family travels, the journey continues online.
