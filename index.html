The Odyssey Way

A private passage from age fifteen to the world's leading universities

Odyssey is a hybrid homeschooling programme for students aged 14 and over. In place of a traditional international high school, students earn U.S. college credits online, study four focused hours a day at The Great Room in Gaysorn Tower, and are guided, one to one, towards entering a top U.S. university as a junior by eighteen.

A different rhythm

Four hours a day.
Not twelve.

Odyssey replaces the long days of exam preparation with four concentrated hours of university-level study. The rest of the day belongs to the student: sport, art, research, rest. Each student moves at their own pace, typically completing the journey in two to three years.
