How mentorship works

Personal, from the first conversation to the final application

I

Understand

Each mentor begins by understanding the student: strengths, interests, gaps and goals.

II

Plan

Together they set goals and a pathway, drawing on U.S. and U.K. admissions strategy and the best options in Asia.

III

Reinforce

Regular sessions revisit and reinforce what matters, so progress is steady and every step is understood.
